# Privacy Policy for Speech Relay by Topitot

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Speech Relay by Topitot is designed to help users communicate by tapping words and symbols. This app is built to work locally on your device.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.

The app may store content that you or a caregiver enters into the app on the device itself, such as:
- board customizations
- photos added for communication cells
- saved phrases and settings

This information stays on the device unless you choose to remove it.

## How We Use Information

Any information stored in the app is used only to provide the app's features, such as:
- showing your communication board
- saving custom cells and settings
- playing spoken output on the device

## Third-Party Services

Speech Relay by Topitot does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Speech Relay by Topitot is intended for use by children and caregivers. Because the app does not collect personal data off the device, there is no child data sent to us.

## Device Permissions

The app may request permissions needed for its features, such as access to photos or files when you choose to add custom images. These permissions are used only for the feature you selected.

## Security

Because your data stays on your device, its protection depends on the security of your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the repository.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the GitHub Issues page for the repository.
