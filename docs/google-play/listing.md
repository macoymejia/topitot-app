# Speech Relay by Topitot - Play Store Listing

## Short Description (80 chars max)
AAC communication board for children and speech-delayed users. Tap words, build sentences, speak out loud.

## Full Description
Speech Relay by Topitot helps children and people with speech difficulties communicate by tapping visual communication cells.

Build sentences word by word using a simple 5x5 grid of core vocabulary words, food, people, actions, feelings, and places. When ready, tap speak and hear the device say your words out loud.

Features:
- Offline-first communication board that works anywhere without internet
- Portrait-first design built for phones and tablets
- 5x5 grid of large, easy-to-tap communication cells
- Folder navigation up to 4 levels deep for organizing vocabulary
- Customizable cells with your own words, symbols, colors, and photos
- Sentence strip at the top with speak, undo, and clear controls
- Reset action to restore the default starter vocabulary
- Text-to-speech playback using your device voice
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking

Perfect for:
- Children learning to communicate
- Speech-delayed users who need AAC support
- Caregivers, teachers, and therapists supporting communication
- Anyone who benefits from visual sentence building

Your data stays on your device. No personal information is collected, stored, or shared.
